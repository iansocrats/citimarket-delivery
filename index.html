Web vs. Phone Architecture: Handling Real-World Nairobi Logistics

How CitiMarket Delivery solves battery consumption, network dropouts in concrete buildings, and desktop-to-mobile synchronization across 4 device types.

📡

1. Offline-First Sync & CRDTs (Courier Phone)

Nairobi cell service drops in basement parking or building lobbies. The courier mobile app uses an encrypted local SQLite write-ahead queue. The rider confirms delivery offline; the signed receipt synchronizes automatically with exponential backoff once 4G returns.

2. Kalman GPS Filter & Dead Reckoning

Continuous GPS pings drain courier phone batteries in 90 minutes. CitiMarket transmits telemetry only when the courier moves $>15$m or turns $>20^\circ$. The customer phone uses client-side Dead Reckoning to smoothly interpolate the motorcycle icon along curves at 60fps.

Engineering Architecture

Engineering Concepts Powering CitiMarket Delivery

The technical foundations ensuring CitiMarket Delivery scales from 50 orders a day to 50,000 without server crashes, ghost couriers, lost payments, or wilted flowers.

1. Spatial Data Structures Uber H3 / PostGIS R-Tree

Hexagonal Hierarchical Spatial Indexing

Instead of calculating costly distance formulas across all drivers ($O(N)$), Nairobi is partitioned into discrete H3 hexagonal cells. Finding all active couriers around City Market is an $O(1)$ constant-time lookup.

⚡ Importance to Business & Operations:

Eliminates database freezes during peak surges (Valentine’s Day, Mother’s Day). Queries respond in under 4 milliseconds regardless of rider volume.

2. Operations Research Kuhn-Munkres / Bipartite

Maximum-Weight Bipartite Dispatch Engine

Computes an optimal matching between all pending orders and active riders in the dispatch batch, minimizing total fleet transit time and flower dehydration risk.

⚡ Importance to Business & Operations:

Cuts fleet travel times by 35% and lowers rider fuel burn. Keeping stems hydrated eliminates wilted return claims.

3. Concurrency Control Redis Redlock & OCC

Atomic Gig Claiming & Optimistic Locking

Prevents race conditions where two riders claim the same bouquet simultaneously using Redis atomic primitives with a lease TTL and Optimistic Concurrency Control (`version_id`).

⚡ Importance to Business & Operations:

Guarantees zero double-assignments and prevents rider conflict at City Market stalls.

4. Distributed Transactions Saga Orchestrator

Saga Pattern with Automated Compensating Reversals

Maintains data consistency across Safaricom M-Pesa, florist inventory, and courier dispatch. If an edge case occurs, automated reversals refund the buyer instantly.

⚡ Importance to Business & Operations:

Protects customer trust. If courier matching fails, money is automatically refunded within seconds via M-Pesa B2C.
