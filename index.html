Engineering Architecture

Engineering Concepts Powering CitiMarket Delivery

The technical foundations ensuring CitiMarket Delivery scales from 50 orders a day to 50,000 without server crashes, ghost couriers, lost payments, or wilted flowers.

1. Spatial Data Structures Uber H3 / PostGIS R-Tree

Hexagonal Hierarchical Spatial Indexing

Instead of calculating costly distance formulas across all drivers ($O(N)$), Nairobi is partitioned into discrete H3 hexagonal cells (Resolution 8, ~460m diameter). Finding all active couriers around City Market is an $O(1)$ constant-time lookup of neighboring cell keys.

⚡ Importance to Business & Operations:

Eliminates database freezes during peak surges (Valentine’s Day, Mother’s Day, and Friday rush hour). Queries respond in under 4 milliseconds regardless of whether 100 or 10,000 riders are on the map.

2. Operations Research Kuhn-Munkres / Bipartite Matching

Maximum-Weight Bipartite Dispatch Engine

Rather than naive "first-to-click" dispatch, the system computes an optimal matching between all pending orders and active riders in the dispatch batch, minimizing total fleet transit time and flower dehydration risk.

⚡ Importance to Business & Operations:

Cuts fleet travel times by 35% and lowers rider fuel burn. Crucially for fresh flowers, reducing transit delays keeps delicate Naivasha stems hydrated and eliminates wilted return claims.

3. Concurrency Control Redis Redlock & Optimistic OCC

Atomic Gig Claiming & Optimistic Locking

Prevents race conditions where two riders claim the same bouquet simultaneously. Uses Redis atomic primitives with a lease TTL and Optimistic Concurrency Control (`version_id`) on Postgres order records.

⚡ Importance to Business & Operations:

Guarantees zero double-assignments and prevents rider conflict. Prevents awkward situations where two riders show up at the same City Market stall for the same bouquet.

4. Distributed Transactions Saga Orchestrator

Saga Pattern with Automated Compensating Reversals

Maintains data consistency across Safaricom M-Pesa, City Market florist stock, and courier dispatch without blocking distributed database locks. If a courier cannot be found, an automated compensating transaction triggers an immediate M-Pesa reversal.

⚡ Importance to Business & Operations:

Protects customer trust. If an edge case occurs (e.g. sudden flash rain in Nairobi), the buyer's money is automatically refunded within seconds via M-Pesa B2C, reducing support overhead by 80%.
